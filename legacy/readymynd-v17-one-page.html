Textile DPP & Bangladesh

Under the EU's Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR), DPP makes product identity, materials, manufacturing, and care details available to authorised users. Bangladesh manufacturers can use this preparation window to optimize product records and data governance.

Proactive preparation before strict enforcement.

Assessment Service Overview

"Know Your Readiness Before You Invest." ReadyMynd provides an independent diagnostic examining how your factory manages product info, supplier records, traceability, and digital systems.

Service Overview & Focus

The assessment examines how a factory manages product info, supplier records, manufacturing documentation, traceability, and internal responsibilities. ReadyMynd reviews available info, identifies readiness gaps, and recommends practical improvements for management.

2.2 Policy Rule

Scope & Coverage: "One Product. One Factory."

The factory selects one representative garment style or product, and our assessment evaluates relevant info and processes within one physical manufacturing facility. Includes Tech Packs, BOMs, material composition, supplier records, production logs, quality documents, and ERP/spreadsheet systems.

2.3

Eligibility & Requirements

Open to all Bangladesh garment factories able to select a style and appoint an internal coordinator. Complete digital records are not required—identifying missing info is part of the diagnostic.

2.4

Assessment Benefits

Provides a structured view of current readiness, highlights compliance vulnerabilities before buyers find them, supports internal department coordination, and guides smart investment priorities.

Boundary Notice

Exclusions & Limitations

ReadyMynd's readiness assessment is an independent diagnostic service—not a DPP certification, formal regulatory compliance audit, or software sales exercise. It does not cover an entire product portfolio, multiple factories, or guarantee future EU compliance. Corrective actions and software implementations are outside standard assessment scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in the readiness assessment?

Export-oriented RMG manufacturers in Bangladesh (knitwear, woven, denim, sweater, composite) wanting to evaluate their product data and DPP readiness.

What does "One Product. One Factory." mean?

The factory selects one representative garment style, and our assessment examines relevant information and processes for that product within one physical facility.

Is this an official EU compliance certification?

No. It is an independent diagnostic readiness assessment designed to help management identify gaps and prepare before final EU rules take effect.

How long does the assessment take?

A typical readiness assessment takes approximately 10–15 working days from questionnaire distribution to management discussion and final report delivery.

Do we need an existing ERP or DPP software platform?

No. The assessment evaluates existing product data regardless of whether it resides in spreadsheets, physical Tech Packs, or complex software.
